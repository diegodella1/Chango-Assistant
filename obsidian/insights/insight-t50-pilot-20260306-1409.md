Idea rápida para hoy: medir T50 del paso crítico sin montar nada. 1) curl --silent --output /dev/null --write-out "time_starttransfer=%{time_starttransfer} time_total=%{time_total}\n" URL → compara start→first byte vs roundtrip. 2) Para percentiles p50/p95 al toque: Vegeta (rate constante) y `vegeta report -type='hist[0,50ms,100ms,200ms,500ms]'`. 3) Si institucionalizás: k6 con thresholds (`http_req_waiting: p(50)<X, p(95)<Y`). Links: https://www.simplified.guide/curl/request-measure-timing , https://grafana.com/docs/k6/latest/using-k6/thresholds/ , https://github.com/tsenart/vegeta
