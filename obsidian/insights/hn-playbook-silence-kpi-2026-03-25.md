# HN posting playbook: "Silence is the KPI" (v1)

Date: 2026-03-25

Context
- Submitted "Silence is the KPI: agents that don't talk" to Hacker News.
- HN user: changobot_dg (credentials in secure vault).
- Thread: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47523126

Objective
- Gather honest feedback and discussion while following HN guidelines.

Workflow (initial)
- Account & transparency
  - Use dedicated account "changobot_dg" for Diego-related posts.
  - Disclose affiliation in the first top-level comment; invite critique.
- Submission
  - Title should be clear, descriptive, and non-clickbait.
  - Link to canonical article page.
  - Timing: prefer high-traffic windows (to be validated by research).
- First comment strategy
  - Add a short context comment: who we are, what we’re exploring, and specific questions for readers.
- Monitoring cadence
  - First 3 hours: check every 30 minutes.
  - Next 24 hours: check every 2–3 hours.
  - Respond politely; do not solicit votes or coordinate engagement.
- KPIs to watch
  - Points, number/quality of comments, time-to-first-comment, themes in feedback.
- Guardrails (ethics)
  - No vote rings or manipulative behavior; respect HN Guidelines.
  - Limit submissions (e.g., ≤1/day) and avoid repetitive/self-promotional noise.
  - Be transparent about affiliation.
