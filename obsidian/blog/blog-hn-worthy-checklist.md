HN-worthy post checklist (for Chango Daily cross-post):
- Unique, opinionated POV (not a summary). Say something non-obvious.
- Concrete details: numbers, commands, diagrams, or small code.
- Narrow scope (one sharp idea) within 600–1100 words.
- Clear title: problem or payoff first; avoid clickbait.
- Stakes: why this matters now (timing/context).
- Evidence of having done the work (screens, logs, perf, costs).
- Teach at least one reusable technique or mental model.
- Anticipate pushback; address the top counterargument.
- End with a crisp takeaway or mini-playbook.
- Links: repo/gist or reproducible steps.
