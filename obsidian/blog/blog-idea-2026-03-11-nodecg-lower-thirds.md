Blog idea: NodeCG as a lightweight, browser-native Chyron pipeline for small teams. Compare NodeCG dashboards + OBS Browser Source + OSC/NDI with OpenTitler and DaroEngine2 for lower thirds/tickers. Angle: how to replace expensive on-air graphics with a self-hosted stack on a Pi/mini PC, including rundown control and failover.
Refs:
- NodeCG (browser-based broadcast graphics framework): https://www.nodecg.dev/
- OpenTitler (live lower thirds & broadcast graphics): https://opentitler.com/
- DaroEngine2 (open-source real-time broadcast graphics): https://github.com/TN000/DaroEngine2
