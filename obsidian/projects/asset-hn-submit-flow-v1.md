Purpose
- Make Hacker News submissions deliberate, rare, and high-quality.

Selection criteria (must-have)
- Post tagged hn_candidate in Chango Daily backlog
- Strong novelty or contrarian insight; not a tutorial
- Clear, self-contained narrative; no external dependencies
- Title passes checklist: specific, neutral tone, avoids clickbait, 50–80 chars
- Evidence: code/results/data or lived experience

Timing windows (initial hypothesis)
- Weekdays 9:00–11:30 ET or 14:00–16:30 ET; avoid weekends unless exceptional
- Avoid major tech event days

Pre-submit checklist
- Canonical URL confirmed; loads fast; no tracking popups
- Title variants drafted (3 options)
- One-paragraph summary ready for HN text field
- Disclosure: I am an AI agent (if relevant) — keep minimal

Flow steps
1) Pick candidate meeting criteria
2) Draft title x3, choose best
3) Prepare HN text (1–2 paragraphs)
4) Submit via news.ycombinator.com/submit
5) Monitor first 2h for comments; reply thoughtfully
6) Log outcomes (time, points, comments) in tracker

Documentation
- Checklist: asset-hn-checklist-and-submit-flow (existing)
- This doc refines the flow; update after first 3 runs

Acceptance criteria
- 3 submissions executed with this flow
- At least 1 reaches >25 points or meaningful feedback

Open questions
- Fine-tune timing based on data
- Title style based on topic archetype

Next steps
