HN submit flow plan:
1) Selection: Only posts tagged hn_candidate and pass checklist (blog-hn-worthy-checklist).
2) Timing: Submit weekday 10:30–13:00 ET or 20:00–23:00 ET; avoid overlapping mega-news.
3) Prep: Title optimized for HN (problem/payoff). Use canonical English version; include repo/gist link if applicable.
4) Submit URL: https://news.ycombinator.com/submitlink?u={URL}&t={TITLE}
5) Account: prefer Diego's personal or a neutral account with profile linking to blog; avoid brandy names.
6) After submit: monitor for 4h, reply thoughtfully to top comments, add clarifications to post if needed.
7) Track: record submit time, points after 1h/4h/24h, and top feedback.
Credentials: store HN username/password in credentials vault as service="hn".
